# Copyright and licensing

ClipForge is for recordings the creator owns or has permission to use. The local import flow requires the user to confirm authorization before processing.

The confirmation is a lightweight safeguard, not legal clearance. It does not establish rights to third-party music, game assets, overlays, voice chat, or other material captured in a recording. Review platform rules and the applicable licence before publication. Keep source attribution with the project when the licence requires it. If a rights concern is reported, stop publication and remove generated outputs that rely on the disputed recording.

ClipForge does not scrape or download other creators' videos. Originals are never modified by the app.
